Echo is a gentle personal journal app—quietly capture the moment, revisit it later as an echo.

✦ Local-first & private
No account required. Entries, photos, tasks, and settings stay on your device by default—not on our servers. No social feed, no public sharing.

✦ Capture the moment
• Echoes: journal with photos, weather & mood
• Drift bottle: open a random past entry
• Mood books: browse by year and mood
• Photo wall: weekly/monthly grids, export images

✦ Gentle reminders
• Tasks with reminders & repeats
• Letters to the future: deliver on a chosen date
• Important days: birthdays & anniversaries

✦ Echo Tree & stats
Track growth, collect charms, and explore mood trends over time.

✦ Echo Plus (optional)
Unlock all photo wall and stationery skins via Google Play subscription or lifetime purchase. Core journaling, tasks, export, and app lock stay free.

Summaries and keywords are generated on-device—no external AI.
